# AIZIX — Branding

## Name

**AIZIX** — short, memorable, tech-forward; implies AI + indexing + edge (“zix” energy).

## Tagline

**Intelligence. Precision. Profit.**

Use on marketing surfaces, login hero, and email footers. Keep the three words in that order for rhythm.

## Voice

- Confident, institutional, never hypey.
- Lead with risk awareness and controls; profit is earned, not promised.
- Prefer “signals,” “regime,” “allocation,” and “paper trading” over slang.

## Color system

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|--------|
| Deep Black | `#060816` | Primary background, depth |
| Neon Purple | `#8A2BE2` | Brand accents, primary CTAs, glow |
| Electric Blue | `#0066FF` | Data highlights, links, charts |
| Cyan | `#00F0FF` | Live states, AI emphasis, borders |
| Profit Green | `#00FFA3` | Positive PnL, success, “bull” hints |

## Typography

- **UI:** geometric sans (e.g. system UI stack or Geist) — clean, investor-grade.
- **Numbers:** tabular lining figures for balances and percentages.

## Iconography

- Minimal line icons; cyan/purple duotone on dark.
- Bull motif for **market mood**; whale silhouette for **large-holder activity**; bot glyph for **automation status**.

## Motion

- Subtle: 150–250ms ease; gradient sheen on primary buttons only.
- Avoid distracting loops on charts in v1.

## Do / Don’t

**Do:** dark glass panels, thin borders, generous whitespace, one hero CTA per view.  
**Don’t:** rainbow gradients, meme stock tickers, “guaranteed returns” copy.
